About Book Explorer

Book Explorer is a website that helps readers discover books from different categories, such as romance, science, history, fantasy, and business.

Our Features

  • Explore different books.
  • Search for books.
  • View book information and covers.
  • Save favorite books.

Technologies Used

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and the Open Library API.

Our Goal

Our goal is to make discovering books easy and enjoyable while practicing web development skills.
